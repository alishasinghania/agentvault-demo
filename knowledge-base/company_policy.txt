MERIDIAN CORP — COMPANY POLICIES AND DATA HANDLING STANDARDS
Version 4.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024 | Approved by: Chief Compliance Officer

================================================================
SECTION 1: DATA CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING
================================================================

1.1 Data Classification Levels

Meridian Corp classifies all company data into four tiers:

TIER 1 — PUBLIC: Marketing materials, press releases, published financial reports.
No handling restrictions apply.

TIER 2 — INTERNAL: Internal memos, project documentation, non-sensitive employee
communications. Access restricted to Meridian Corp employees. Do not share externally
without manager approval.

TIER 3 — CONFIDENTIAL: Customer data, financial projections, HR records, salary
information, strategic plans. Access on a need-to-know basis. Must be encrypted in
transit and at rest. Requires manager sign-off for external sharing.

TIER 4 — RESTRICTED: Credentials, authentication tokens, cryptographic keys,
merger/acquisition information, regulatory filings pre-disclosure. Access limited to
named individuals. Must never be transmitted via unencrypted channels. Requires
VP-level approval and legal review for any sharing.

1.2 Data Retention

Customer records: 7 years post-contract end
Employee records: Duration of employment plus 5 years
Financial records: 10 years per regulatory requirement
Audit logs: 3 years minimum
Ephemeral credentials: Rotate every 90 days, revoke within 24 hours of employee departure

1.3 Data Storage Requirements

All Tier 3 and Tier 4 data must be stored in approved Meridian Corp systems only.
Personal devices are not approved storage locations for Tier 3 or Tier 4 data.
Cloud storage outside the approved Meridian Corp cloud tenancy (AWS us-east-1, eu-west-1)
is prohibited for Tier 3 and above.

================================================================
SECTION 2: ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
================================================================

2.1 Approved Use of Company Systems

Company systems and infrastructure are provided for business purposes. Incidental
personal use is permitted provided it does not:
- Consume excessive bandwidth or storage
- Expose company systems to security risk
- Violate any other section of this policy

2.2 Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited on all Meridian Corp systems:
- Installation of unauthorized software without IT approval
- Sharing credentials with colleagues (each user must have individual credentials)
- Using company systems to access, store, or distribute illegal content
- Circumventing security controls including VPNs, firewalls, or authentication systems
- Mining cryptocurrency or other non-business computational tasks
- Accessing systems or data outside the scope of your role
- Using AI tools not approved by the Technology Governance Committee

2.3 Approved AI Tools

The Technology Governance Committee maintains an approved AI tool list. As of Q1 2024:
- AgentVault (internal) — approved for all employees
- GitHub Copilot — approved for Engineering and Data Science
- All other AI tools require explicit TGC approval before use

Employees are reminded that data entered into any AI tool may be used for model
training by the tool provider unless contractual protections are in place. Only
approved tools have verified data processing agreements.

================================================================
SECTION 3: SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE
================================================================

3.1 Reporting Obligations

All employees must report suspected security incidents within 2 hours of discovery.
Report to: security@meridian.com or call the Security Hotline at ext. 5-SECURE.

A security incident includes but is not limited to:
- Loss or theft of company devices
- Credential compromise or suspected unauthorized access
- Exposure of Tier 3 or Tier 4 data to unauthorized parties
- Malware or ransomware infections
- Unexpected system behavior suggesting compromise

3.2 Incident Classification

P1 — CRITICAL: Active breach, ransomware, credential exposure affecting production systems
P2 — HIGH: Suspected breach, data exposure, significant system compromise
P3 — MEDIUM: Policy violation, minor data exposure, unsuccessful attack attempts
P4 — LOW: Suspicious activity with no confirmed impact

3.3 Employee Obligations During Incidents

Do NOT attempt to remediate incidents independently. Preserve evidence by not
powering off affected systems unless instructed by the Security team. Do not
discuss suspected incidents on non-secure channels.

================================================================
SECTION 4: THIRD-PARTY AND VENDOR POLICY
================================================================

4.1 Vendor Access

Third-party vendors requiring access to Meridian Corp systems must:
- Complete the vendor security assessment questionnaire
- Sign a Data Processing Agreement
- Operate under the principle of least privilege
- Provide contact details for their security team

4.2 AI and Machine Learning Vendors

Vendors providing AI or ML services to Meridian Corp must demonstrate:
- SOC 2 Type II compliance or equivalent
- Data residency guarantees matching Meridian Corp requirements
- Contractual prohibition on training models on Meridian Corp data
- Incident notification within 72 hours of any data exposure

================================================================
SECTION 5: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES
================================================================

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination of employment, and where applicable, referral to law enforcement.

The severity of disciplinary action will be commensurate with the severity of the
violation, whether it was intentional, and any prior violations on record.
