CHAPTER 3: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
================================================================

3.1 Pay Schedule

Employees are paid bi-weekly, with payroll processing on Fridays. Direct deposit
is strongly encouraged and can be configured in the HR Portal (hr.meridian.internal).
Paper checks are available but take an additional 2 business days.

3.2 Health Benefits

Meridian Corp offers three health plan tiers:
- MERIDIAN SELECT: HMO plan, lowest employee premium
- MERIDIAN PLUS: PPO plan, broader network access
- MERIDIAN ELITE: PPO plan with dental and vision included, HSA-compatible

Benefit elections take effect the first of the month following your start date.
Open enrollment occurs each November for January 1 effective dates.

3.3 Retirement

Meridian Corp 401(k) Plan:
- Immediate eligibility upon hire
- Company match: 100% of the first 2%, 50% of the next 2% (up to 4% total)
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested at 4 years
- Investment options managed by Fidelity — contact fidelity.com/meridian for support

3.4 Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates:
- Years 0–2: 15 days per year (accrued monthly)
- Years 3–5: 20 days per year
- Years 6+: 25 days per year

Additionally:
- 10 company holidays per year (published in January)
- 5 sick days per year (separate from PTO, does not accrue)
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregiver, 6 weeks for secondary

3.5 Additional Benefits

- Annual learning stipend: $2,000 for courses, conferences, books, certifications
- Home office stipend: $500 one-time for remote employees
- Cell phone reimbursement: $50/month for roles requiring mobile access
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free confidential counseling, 8 sessions/year
- Employee stock purchase plan: 15% discount, enrollment each February

CHAPTER 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================

4.1 Our Values

INTEGRITY: We do what we say. We say what we do. We don't cut corners when no one
is watching — especially then.

RESPECT: Every person at Meridian Corp deserves to be treated with dignity. This
extends to customers, partners, vendors, and competitors.

EXCELLENCE: Good enough isn't. We take pride in our work and hold each other to
high standards — supportively.

TRANSPARENCY: We share information openly and honestly, even when it's uncomfortable.

4.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any relationship (financial, personal, or professional) that
could influence — or appear to influence — their decisions on behalf of Meridian Corp.
Disclosures go to your manager and HR at ethics@meridian.com.

Examples of disclosures required:
- Ownership stake in a vendor we do or might do business with
- Close personal relationship with a direct report
- Outside employment or consulting that overlaps with Meridian Corp business

4.3 Anti-Harassment Policy

Meridian Corp maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any kind, including:
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome advances
- Harassment based on race, gender, religion, national origin, disability, age,
  sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
- Bullying, intimidation, or creating a hostile work environment

Report concerns to HR directly or via the anonymous Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-ETHIC.

4.4 Social Media

When posting on personal social media:
- Do not disclose confidential Meridian Corp information
- Do not represent personal opinions as official Meridian Corp positions
- Add "Views are my own" disclaimers when discussing industry topics
- Employees in IR, Legal, or Marketing roles should review their additional guidelines

CHAPTER 5: WORKPLACE AND REMOTE WORK
================================================================

5.1 Office Locations

Headquarters: 350 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94105
Engineering Hub: 200 Water Street, Austin, TX 78701
European Office: 12 Finsbury Square, London, EC2A 1AS
APAC Office: 8 Shenton Way, Singapore 068811

5.2 Remote Work Policy

Meridian Corp operates as a hybrid company. Teams set their own in-office expectations
within these guidelines:
- Minimum 2 days in-office per week for roles classified as "hybrid"
- Fully remote roles are designated at hire and require manager + VP approval to change
- Core collaboration hours are 10am–3pm in your primary timezone

5.3 Equipment

New employees receive a standard equipment package:
- MacBook Pro (Engineering) or MacBook Air (all other roles)
- Company-issued peripherals (monitor, keyboard, mouse) for remote employees
- Equipment remains Meridian Corp property and must be returned upon departure

CHAPTER 6: PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT
================================================================

6.1 Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews occur twice per year:
- Mid-year review: July (developmental focus)
- Annual review: January (compensation decisions)

The performance framework uses four ratings:
- Exceeds Expectations: Consistently delivers above and beyond
- Meets Expectations: Delivers on all commitments reliably
- Developing: Working toward expectations with a clear improvement plan
- Below Expectations: Serious concerns requiring immediate action

6.2 Career Development

Each employee has a development plan updated annually in partnership with their manager.
Internal mobility is encouraged — open positions are posted internally 1 week before
external posting. The Learning Portal offers 5,000+ courses at no employee cost.

================================================================
CHAPTER 7: LEAVING MERIDIAN CORP
================================================================

Notice periods:
- Individual contributor: 2 weeks minimum
- Manager or above: 4 weeks strongly requested

On your final day, you must return all equipment to IT and complete the exit checklist
with HR. Access to systems is revoked at end-of-business on your last day.

Non-compete and non-solicit agreements, if applicable, are outlined in your offer letter
and employment agreement.
